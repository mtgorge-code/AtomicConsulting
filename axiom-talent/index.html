The route, in order

Four stops. One firm handles all of them.

  1. 01

    Recruit & place

    We match IT and technical professionals with Canadian employers actively hiring — not a job board, a search run against real open roles, with a recruiter who stays in the loop through the offer.

  2. 02

    Immigrate & settle

    Work permits, PR pathways, and the paperwork that comes with either. We file it, track it, and tell you what's actually happening instead of leaving you refreshing a portal.

  3. 03

    Advise employers

    For companies: HR process work and guidance on where AI genuinely helps a hiring pipeline — screening, scheduling, structured interviews — and where it doesn't.

  4. 04

    Invest & build

    Once you're earning in Canada, we help you put that income to work — registered accounts, dividend income strategies, and a plan that isn't generic off-the-shelf advice.

Why one firm

Most people relocating for work deal with four different companies that don't talk to each other.

A recruiter who disappears after the offer. An immigration consultant who's never spoken to your employer. A bank that doesn't know you just landed. Axiom runs recruitment, immigration filing, employer advisory, and investment planning as one file, not four handoffs.

Our story

Why Axiom exists

Nikhilesh Ravi built Axiom out of his own move to Canada — the version of it that actually happens, not the version on a settlement agency's brochure. A recruiter who stopped returning calls after the offer letter. An immigration file that sat in a queue with no one able to explain why. A bank that had no idea what to do with income that started three weeks ago in a new country.

None of those problems were really about paperwork. They were about nobody owning the whole thing. Every stop in the process had a different person, a different company, a different incentive — and the person moving countries was the only one who had to hold it all together.

Axiom is what he wished existed: one firm, one point of contact, from the first interview to the first investment account. It's built for the person who's done this the hard way once already and shouldn't have to again — and for the employers who are hiring that person and want a partner who understands both sides of the table.

— Nikhilesh Ravi, Founder
